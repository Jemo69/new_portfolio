# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, both served by the same surface:

- **Prospective clients and employers** landing on the site to judge capability. They skim fast, scan projects, and want a clear path to contact.
- **Developers and design-curious readers** coming for the blog. They want long-form content that is fast to read and credible in craft.

## Product Purpose

JEMO's personal portfolio: prove skill through work, share ideas through writing, and convert both into a direct line to Jeremy. Success looks like a visitor leaving with a concrete impression of capability, and a low-friction way to start a conversation (mailto).

## Brand Personality

Heavy, reliable, unapologetic. The JEMO CORE (encase) voice: a tactical monolith. Binary contrast, hard containment, structural uppercase labels, softened industrial geometry. The site should read like a control panel, not a brochure.

## Anti-references

- The site's own previous "Adaptive Materiality" system: indigo/lavender palette, radial-gradient hero glow, liquid-glass navbar blur, pill-shaped tags, squircle clip-paths, tiny tracked uppercase eyebrows ("CAPABILITIES", "PORTFOLIO", "BLOG") above every section.
- Any softness-as-polish trope: gradient text, glassmorphism as default, ghost-card shadows (1px border + wide blur), over-rounded corners (24px+), cream/sand/warm-neutral body backgrounds, thin-line icons, sketchy SVG illustration.

## Design Principles

- **Binary contrast**: if it's not black, it's white. Gray is a utility, not a personality. Color is reserved for content (imagery), never for structure.
- **Hard containment**: every element lives in a defined space; borders crate information instead of whitespace alone.
- **Structural voice**: nav items, button labels, and modal headers are UPPERCASE and BOLD; body copy is sentence case and reserved for meaning.
- **Softened industrial**: harsh contrast, approachable geometry, consistent 8-12px radii on containers, no pill shapes on large containers.
- **Evidence over assertion**: the work and the writing carry the claim; no marketing filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the floor; black/white pairing trivially exceeds it (~19.5:1). Muted links on black must still hold ≥4.5:1. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected: no layout animation, no content gated behind transitions. Uppercase is reserved for short structural labels only, never body copy.
